n-test-reviewer — colour theme directions

Five palettes for a calm study tool: a parent photographs a returned school test, AI generates fresh Practice Tests, the student takes them in-app. Each direction is shown as MUI semantic tokens plus three real surfaces — Student Home, Student Results, Parent Analytics. Explicitly not gamified ed-tech: no streaks, badges, mascots, points or cheerleading.

Accessibility floor: WCAG 2.1 AA on student-facing surfaces. Every graded answer state pairs colour + icon + text label — never colour alone. Contrast ratios for text-on-background are stated per palette and in the <style> comments.

Dark mode is an open decision — the right-hand column of every variation is a preview only.

VARIATION 1

Study Desk

Warm paper, editorial ink — a quiet desk lamp and a printed workbook. Steady, unhurried, faintly old-fashioned.

Warmth without sweetness. The cream ground keeps a wrong answer from feeling clinical; ink-blue primary keeps it from feeling babyish.
