# Epic 4 Context: Practice Test Generation, Review & Release

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

Turn a committed Source Test's Extraction into fresh practice material a parent has personally approved. A parent chooses how many Practice Tests to generate (optionally weighted toward one Topic), watches an async job produce them, then reads every generated Question, edits or deletes the bad ones, optionally sets a countdown timer, and releases the draft to a Student Profile — or discards it. This epic is the product's mandatory human quality gate: nothing generated by AI may reach a child without a parent having seen it, and no path may auto-release. It is also where the Generation Allowance is actually spent, so correct charging and honest cost copy are part of the epic, not an afterthought.

## Stories

- Story 4.1: Practice Test Generation (bounded, priced, async)
- Story 4.2: Topic-Weighted Regeneration
- Story 4.3: Draft Review
- Story 4.4: Draft Editing
- Story 4.5: Release or Discard
- Story 4.6: Optional Timer Configuration

## Requirements & Constraints

- Generation runs from a persisted Extraction, never from stored image bytes — regeneration must keep working after Page Images expire, and must never require a re-upload.
- Per-request maximum is 5 Practice Tests, further bounded by the account's remaining Generation Allowance. Counts above the remaining allowance stay **visible but disabled with the reason stated** — never silently trimmed from the list.
- The bound is enforced server-side on the generation request and clamped independently of what the client sent. UI disablement alone does not satisfy the requirement.
- Cost is stated **before** the parent confirms, denominated in Practice Tests (never credits or abstract units), naming both what the action spends and what remains. Same rule for the weighted-regeneration action.
- Each generated Practice Test defaults its Question count from the Source Test's own count, covers the Extraction's Topics, and reproduces its Question Format mix proportionally.
- Multiple Practice Tests from one Source Test must be materially different from one another and from the source — not reorderings, and no verbatim copies of a source question.
- Every generated Question carries its correct answer, its Topic(s), and, for Multiple Choice, plausible distractors.
- Any fraction in a generated question, answer option, or correct answer is emitted in **structured numerator/denominator form**, not an inline string. This is a schema constraint on generation output, not a styling concern — a spoken alternative cannot be recovered from a glyph.
- Weighted regeneration produces Questions predominantly on the chosen Topic, reuses the originating Source Test with no new upload, and is bounded, clamped, and priced exactly like unweighted generation. This epic ships only the capability; the Analytics dashboard and missed-question drill-down entry points are wired in Epic 7.
- Generation is asynchronous: the parent may leave the screen and return; progress is visible; nothing is lost by leaving.
- Generation failure leaves the Source Test intact and retryable without re-upload.
- Draft review must show **every** Question with its correct answer, distractors, and Topic in one reviewable list. A draft never appears in Student Mode.
- An edited Question is exactly what the student sees and is graded against. Editing is available in draft state only.
- Questions can be deleted down to one remaining. Deleting the last Question discards the Practice Test, and the confirmation must say so in words **before** the action, including that the already-spent Generation Allowance is not refunded.
- Release transitions the draft to released and makes it immediately visible in that Student Profile's Student Mode. Release is irreversible in v0 and a released Practice Test can no longer be edited.
- Discard removes the Practice Test from all student-facing surfaces and excludes it from Analytics.
- Timer is optional and **off by default**, with a suggested duration derived from question count, entered in minutes, editable at any point up to release and never after.
- When enabled, remaining time is available to the student throughout the Attempt, with three **mandatory, non-escalating** pre-expiry warnings at 5 minutes, 1 minute, and 20 seconds. No warning may be more urgent than the one before it in wording, prominence, or audibility.
- On expiry the Attempt auto-submits, auto-submit is announced at the moment it happens, and every unanswered Question is graded incorrect — the single path by which a blank becomes `incorrect` rather than `unanswered`.
- No allowance counter, cost figure, tier label, or model name is ever reachable from a student-scoped surface or endpoint.
- No log line, error report, or cost row may carry generated Question content. Identifiers only.

## Technical Decisions

- `practicetest` owns the Practice Test, Question, Attempt, and Answer entities. Other modules read through its service, never through its Prisma delegate. Generation prompt text lives in this module.
- **Generation is a queued job** on the Postgres-backed queue: the request enqueues and returns, the job outlives the connection, and progress surfaces read **job status** — never a held connection. Enqueueing work and mutating state share one transaction.
- **Generation is incremental and partial success is a real outcome.** Each draft is committed as it lands and charges one Generation Allowance unit; landed drafts are never discarded on a later failure of the same job. A job producing 3 of 5 requested tests reports as *partially complete* and charges 3. A job where nothing landed charges nothing and reports as failed.
- Allowances are **derived, not decremented**: Generation usage = Practice Tests whose status has ever reached draft, counted over the account's period window. A Practice Test charges on first reaching draft and never again, carried as a durable marker on the row. The cap check and the artifact INSERT share one transaction; DB serialization enforces the cap. Deletion never refunds, and a discarded draft does not refund.
- All AI calls go through the single `ai` module (client, pinned model snapshots, retry/timeout policy, cost accounting). Generation is its own call class with its own pinned model snapshot held in configuration, and writes an AiCall row per completed provider call.
- Structured output uses the Responses API with Structured Outputs and a JSON schema built from Zod (`zodTextFormat`) — never Chat Completions `response_format`, never legacy `json_object`. The inherited `chat.completions` pattern from the sibling project is explicitly superseded.
- **Deterministic post-hoc validation runs in code on every generated payload after schema validation.** The canonical case is a Multiple Choice Question whose correct answer is not among its own options — that is malformed and must be caught in code, not trusted because it parsed. Structured fraction shape is likewise a schema-level guarantee.
- AI failures split by fault: an unusable input is a terminal client fault surfaced as a retake prompt; a provider timeout/rate-limit/refusal/outage is an upstream fault retried with backoff. A schema or post-hoc validation failure counts as **upstream**. Generation exhaustion leaves the job failed with whatever drafts landed committed and charged.
- Generated Topic labels go through the single `normalize(label, subjectId)` interface — callers never match Topics by their own rules, or one concept splits into two Mastery values.
- **A released-state write barrier belongs on `practicetest`**: the API must refuse a timer change (and any Question edit) once the Practice Test is released, regardless of what the UI offers. A timer mutated mid-life would retroactively change how expiry graded past Attempts.
- Timer authority at Attempt time is the server, evaluated at submit — this epic only writes the configuration; the countdown rendering and expiry evaluation belong to Epic 5.
- Parent-scoped data is never server-rendered: generation, progress, and draft-review surfaces fetch client-side carrying the in-memory elevation token.
- Every user-facing string is parameterized, never a hardcoded literal; parent-facing copy uses the third person about the student.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- **Generate step → generation progress → draft review** is one continuous flow, with *Pending drafts* as a first-class persistent Parent View destination a returning parent lands on directly.
- The generation-progress screen advises the parent to stay (it is the only live completion signal, since v0 has no notifications) but **must never claim work will be lost** if they leave — that would be false and would contradict retry-without-re-upload and charge-on-success. A parent who leaves finds finished drafts in Pending drafts. The progress step list is exposed as a status live region.
- Draft review shows **one draft Practice Test at a time** as its full question list, with persistent progress context ("draft 2 of 5"). Per-Question edit and delete act **in place** without navigating away.
- Release and discard are **per draft — no batch release.** Released Practice Tests appear on Student Home as each one is released, not all at once.
- Question rows in draft review are paper-role content set in the serif face — generated Question and Explanation content carries that family wherever it appears, including on the parent side.
- The delete-to-zero confirmation says in plain words that the Practice Test is discarded and that the spent Generation Allowance is not refunded.
- Drafts are durable: unreleased drafts persist indefinitely with review position and per-Question edits preserved, and survive the Parent View idle timeout.
- Timer configuration lives with the draft in draft review, pre-filled with the suggested duration. The timer display component uses tabular figures changing value in place with no per-tick animation; at each warning threshold the value takes the warning color **and** gains a text label beside it (never color or motion alone), exposed as a timer with its live region raised at the thresholds only.
- Copy is plain fact — no error codes, no cheerleading, no exclamation marks, no upsell language. The allowance readout names tier, usage, and reset date, always denominated in Practice Tests.
- Accessibility: parent-tier tap targets, every control a real focusable element with an accessible name, visible focus rings, reduced motion honored, and every state change that matters announced with the same words that are displayed.
- Reference mocks: the capture mockup in the UX design's `mockups/` directory covers the generate step and generation progress.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- The whole epic depends on Epic 3's persisted Extraction (Questions, Question Formats, Topics, structured fractions) and on its Source Test / Student Profile classification. A thin Extraction warned about in Epic 3 is the input here.
- Story 4.1 establishes bounding, server-side clamping, cost statement, async job, and incremental charging; Story 4.2 reuses all of it and adds only the Topic weighting.
- Stories 4.3–4.6 all operate on the draft state Story 4.1 produces; Story 4.5's release transition is what closes editing for 4.4 and 4.6.
- Generation Allowance is **counted and charged here, but hard-blocking at cap ships in Epic 9** — this epic must charge correctly and state cost honestly in copy without implementing enforcement.
- Story 4.6 writes timer configuration only; the in-Attempt countdown, offline expiry, auto-submit, and the `incorrect`-vs-`unanswered` grade consequence are Epic 5.
- Released Practice Tests are the input to Epic 5's Student Mode list and Attempts; generated Topic labels feed Epic 7's Topic normalization and Mastery.
- Weighted generation's entry points (the Analytics Weak Area action and the missed-question drill-down) are wired in Epic 7, not here.
- Nothing in generation may couple to stored Page Image bytes — that is what makes Epic 8's 90-day image expiry safe.
